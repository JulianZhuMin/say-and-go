用唔到咪高峰Can't use the microphone

  • iPhone／iPad：請用 Safari 開呢個網頁。去「設定」開啟「Siri」同「聽寫」（設定 → 一般 → 鍵盤 → 啟用聽寫）。Safari 問可唔可以用咪高峰同語音辨識，請撳「允許」。 iPhone/iPad: open this page in Safari. Turn on Siri and Dictation in Settings (Settings → General → Keyboard → Enable Dictation). Tap Allow when Safari asks for the microphone and speech recognition.
  • 電腦：請用 Chrome 或者 Edge，網址列問用咪高峰嗰陣撳「允許」。 Computer: use Chrome or Edge, and allow the microphone when asked.
  • 唔緊要！你都可以撳圖，聽吓飛機、地鐵、巴士、電單車同消防車嘅名。（要講出個名，佢哋先會郁。） You can still tap the pictures to hear their names. (The vehicles only move when the child says the name.)
